# VoiceHelperBot
This is just voice bot for your use!

> [!WARNING]
> There may be many bugs and the AI may not work for some time sometimes!

> ### > All commands:
> | Command | Description |
> | --- | --- |
> | `Привет` | He just say "Hi!" |
> | `Как меня зовут` | He say your computer name |
> | `Открой` | He open a app/site (ondoes not work on some apps. Sorry... will be fixed in future updates) |
> | `Закрой окно`, `закрой приложение`, `закрой программу`, `закрой прогу`| Close your current window |
> | `Сменить микрофон`, `смени микрофон` | Switch microphone (if it's broken yout input: enter to path of bot, open "Settings.json", switch number to 0) |
> | `Удали историю`| Delete your AI talkink history |
> | `Пока`| Close bot |

> [!NOTE]
> 
> For ask AI just say "Бот" and your question, like this:
> "Бот какая классная погода!"
> 
> Before giving the command, say "Bot" like this:
> "Бот привет!"
